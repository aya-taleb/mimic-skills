# Fixes: what Lunchbox Club and Curbside Eats customers hate

Sample: 14 reviews (Lunchbox Club 10, Curbside Eats 4) from google-maps, yelp, reddit. Small sample, a direction not a ranking.
One row flagged as instruction-like text (lunchboxclub review r7) and left out.

## What they hate
1. Rigid ordering: orders lock Sunday 8pm; trucks need 7 days and 2 days to confirm. 4 reviews, 2 sources.
2. Hard to cancel, billed again. 3 reviews, 1 source (thin).
3. Late or cold delivery. 3 reviews (thin).

## What is missing
1. Small group orders under 20. "Wish we could book for a small group." https://maps.google.example/curbsideeats/review/c1
2. More variety. "Same six dishes on rotation." https://maps.google.example/lunchboxclub/review/r3

## Fix plan
| # | fix | size | evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | change or skip until 9am the same day | M | hate #1 |
| 2 | a different partner truck each day | M | missing #2 |
| 3 | office orders from 5 people | S | missing #1 |
| 4 | cancel online, no email | S | hate #2 |
| 5 | pickup at the truck at a set time slot, not a lobby bag | M | hate #3 |

## Angle (recommended)
For downtown office workers tired of the same lunch locked in on Sunday, Noon Wheels brings a different food truck each day and lets you change your order until 9am.
