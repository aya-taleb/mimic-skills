# Tally

- Name: Tally
- One-line pitch: Shared to-do lists for small agency teams that show who is stuck.
- Who it serves: agency owners with 3 to 15 people
- Stage: beta, 212 teams
- Website: tallyapp.io

## Goals
- 50 paying teams by June

## Tone
- Tone words: direct, warm, a bit dry
- Words to avoid: hustle, crush it, ninja, rockstar

## Proof I can use
- 212 beta teams (source: our admin dashboard, October)

## Brand look
- Background #1B2433 (ink navy), text #FFFFFF, accent #E8664F (coral), font Inter

## Off-limits
- client names, revenue numbers
