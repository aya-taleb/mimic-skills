# Lunchbox Club website (saved 2026-10-11 from https://lunchboxclub.example)

Lunch, handled. Pick your meals for the week by Sunday 8pm and we deliver to your office lobby between 11:45 and 12:30, Monday to Friday.

## How it works
1. Choose a plan: 3, 4 or 5 lunches a week.
2. Pick from 6 dishes each day on our app or website. Menus rotate every two weeks.
3. We drop all orders for your building at the lobby at the same time.

## Plans
- 3 lunches a week: $13.50 per meal
- 5 lunches a week: $12.50 per meal
- Delivery: free for buildings with 5+ members, $2.99 per order otherwise
- Billed weekly every Monday. Skip a week by Sunday 8pm. Cancel by emailing support.

## Where we deliver
Downtown Denver, LoDo and RiNo.

## FAQ
- Can I change my order after Sunday? No, orders lock Sunday at 8pm.
- Vegetarian options? Two vegetarian dishes a day. Vegan on Wednesdays.
