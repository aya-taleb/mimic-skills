# Curbside Eats website (saved 2026-10-11 from https://curbsideeats.example)

We bring Denver's best food trucks to your office.

## For offices
- Book a truck for your team lunch. Minimum 20 meals.
- $15 per meal, plus a $75 truck fee for under 40 meals.
- Book at least 7 days ahead with our request form. We confirm within 2 business days.
- Pay by invoice, net 15.

## Rotation
- Our partner network: 25 trucks. Exclusive partners for downtown Denver.
- Monthly "truck of the month" plan for buildings: one truck every Thursday.

## FAQ
- Dietary options depend on the truck. Ask when you book.
- Rain plan: trucks park in your garage if there is room, else we deliver bagged meals.
