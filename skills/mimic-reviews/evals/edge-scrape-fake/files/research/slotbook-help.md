# Slotbook Help Center (saved 2026-10-20 from https://slotbook.example/help)

## Getting started
- Sign up with email or Google.
- Connect Google Calendar or Outlook so Slotbook can see when you are busy.
- Set your weekly hours under Availability. You can add date overrides for holidays.

## Event types
- Create an event type with a name, length (15, 30, 45, 60 minutes or custom) and location (phone, video link, in person).
- Add buffer time before and after meetings.
- Add custom questions to the booking form.
- Limit how many meetings per day.

## Booking page
- Every event type has a public booking page at slotbook.example/<you>/<event>.
- Guests pick a time and fill in their name and email. Guests do not need an account.
- The page shows times in the guest's detected time zone.
- Guests get a confirmation email with links to reschedule or cancel.

## Notifications
- Email reminders 24 hours and 1 hour before. Text (SMS) reminders are on the Teams plan only.

## Payments
- Take payment at booking with Stripe (Pro plan and up).

## Teams
- Round-robin and collective event types on the Teams plan.
- Admin can add seats. Each seat is billed monthly.

## Embeds and integrations
- Embed the booking page on your website with a snippet.
- Zoom and Google Meet links added to invites.
- App marketplace with 40+ partner apps.
