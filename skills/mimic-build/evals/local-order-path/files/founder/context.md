# Noon Wheels

Pitch: a weekly street food lunch plan for office workers in downtown Denver. Food trucks and small restaurants sign up as partners.
Who it serves: office workers and office managers in downtown Denver and LoDo, teams of 10 to 200.
Stage: pre-launch. Solo founder, no developer. Wants to avoid custom code at the start.
Goals: 40 founding members and 5 partner trucks by launch.
Launch: January 15.
Founding offer: 40 founding spots at 20% off the launch price. Pay nothing today: hold a spot now, first payment 7 days before the first order.
Tone: friendly, local, straight. Avoid: "foodie", "game-changing", "curated experience".
Proof the founder can use: 31 people on the waitlist (from the landing page test, counted 2026-10-15).
Off-limits: naming competitors in ads.
