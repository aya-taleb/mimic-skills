Schincariol's founder-pricing and founder-consumer read prices and complaints from it, and
mimic-recon reads the rival list. Plain markdown, no HTML.

Notes:

- `source` holds a full URL. If one row has several facts from different pages, put the
  URLs in the cell separated by spaces.
- Local rivals: add a `distance` column after `name`, and hours in "what they sell".
- If a price is your estimate, write `est. $<x>` and give the reasoning in "Not found".
