# Slotbook pricing (saved 2026-10-20 from https://slotbook.example/pricing)

| plan | price | what you get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 | 1 event type, email reminders |
| Pro | $12 per seat per month, billed yearly ($15 monthly) | unlimited event types, payments, custom questions |
| Teams | $20 per seat per month, billed yearly | round-robin, SMS reminders, admin |

All paid plans renew automatically. Cancel from Billing settings by contacting support.
