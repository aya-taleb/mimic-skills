# Back Street Lunch

Name: Back Street Lunch
One-line pitch: Street food lunch for offices, delivered twice a week.
Who it serves: office managers at companies with 20 to 200 people in downtown Seattle
Stage: launched, 7 offices on one route

## Goals
- a second route with 10 offices by March

## Tone
- Tone words: friendly, plain, local
- Words to avoid: foodie, yummy

## Off-limits
